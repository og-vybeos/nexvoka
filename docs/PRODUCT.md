# Nexvoka — Product Notes

## One-liner

Many voices, one answer. Ask once; Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini deliberate together and hand you one answer, one document, one plan.

## vs. Perplexity (and single-model chat)

Perplexity routes your question to *one* model at a time and adds search. Nexvoka is a different category:

| | Single-model chat / Perplexity | Nexvoka |
|---|---|---|
| Perspectives | One | Three, in shared context |
| Disagreement | None | Structured (Debate/Critique modes) |
| Convergence | N/A | Synthesis → Verdict → one document |
| Actions | Manual | Council-briefed agents (research, delivery, video) |
| Output | A chat log | A decision artifact |

The user doesn't want three answers — they want the answer that survives three AIs arguing about it.

## Target users

1. **Founders/operators** — decision support: pricing, roadmap, GTM. Willing to pay for confidence.
2. **Consultants/agencies** — client-facing deliverables produced by "an AI board of advisors."
3. **Power users** — people already paying for 2-3 AI subscriptions who want them in one place.

## Positioning inside the NextGen/VyBeos portfolio

- A B2C/prosumer SaaS with team-tier expansion (same pattern as SipSense: consumer first, per-seat/per-org tier later).
- White-label potential for the VyBeos multi-tenant pattern: consultancies get "YourBrand Council."

## Pricing hypothesis (validate)

- Free: 3 council sessions/month, 2 rounds max
- Pro $19–29/mo: unlimited sessions, all rounds, document compile, action agents
- Team $49/seat: shared sessions, workspace library of council documents

Council sessions cost 6–12+ model calls, so meter accordingly; margins depend on caching and model-tier routing (cheap models for round 1, frontier for synthesis/verdict is worth testing).

## Brand

- Name: **Nexvoka** — from *nexus* (a point where connections meet) + *voca* (Latin, "to call together"). Many voices called into one. The **k** (for the Latin hard-c sound) makes it distinctive and ownable — nexvoka.com secured for a standard registration rather than a premium/aftermarket price.
- Tagline: **Many voices. One answer.**
- One-line origin story (for public use): *"Nexus plus voca — many voices, one answer."* Keep it to one sentence; confidence is the brand.
- Voice: deliberate, confident, quietly premium. "The council convenes." "The verdict is in."
- Palette: juniper green (#2F5D50), brass (#B08D3E), warm paper (#F7F5F1)
- Type: Fraunces (display) + Inter (body)
- Mark: ◈ (a nexus/diamond — many facets converging)
- Domains: nexvoka.com ✓ secured. (.ai deferred — trademark protects the brand more than a defensive domain does.)
